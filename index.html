About

A Venezuelan chef shaped by Greece, refined by the Gulf. Leonardo García spent two decades moving between kitchens where the Mediterranean is not a concept but a living thing — olive harvests, whole animals, market seasons, and the insistence that technique should serve flavour, not perform over it.

At Attic Restaurant in Bahrain, he built a programme that earned the title of Best Mediterranean Restaurant four consecutive years. At Avli by Tasha's in Dubai's DIFC — Michelin-listed — he led the kitchen through its most celebrated period, translating Greek cuisine for an international audience without softening its edges.

His cooking is direct. The plate does not explain itself. What you taste is the result of decisions made before the ingredient arrived at the pass.
